About Skincare4U

Skincare4U is a skincare assistant that combines AI technology with dermatological expertise. Below you will find our brand identity: the marks we use, what they stand for, and how they are classified.

Brand Form 1

Wortmarke

Skincare4U

The word mark Skincare4U is our primary textual identity. The name combines "Skincare" with "4U" (for you), communicating that every routine is personal and tailored. The compact spelling makes it memorable, searchable, and suitable for digital contexts such as app stores, domain names, and social media handles.

Brand Form 2

Bildmarke

Our figurative mark depicts three skincare product tubes in an illustrative, hand-drawn style. The organic shapes and muted tones convey approachability and authenticity. The illustration is intentionally imperfect, reflecting that skincare is human and errors or mistakes can happen. It works independently of the word mark and remains recognisable at small sizes such as app icons.

Brand Form 3

Farbmarke

Skincare4U Sage Green HEX   #4d7855 RGB   77, 120, 85 HSL   131°, 22%, 39%
Skincare4U Rose Red HEX   #c05858 RGB   192, 88, 88 HSL   0°, 37%, 55%

Our color marks are drawn directly from the Bildmarke: the sage green of the right tube and the muted rose red of the left tube. Green is the primary brand color. It portrays nature and health, perfect for a skincare-focused product. The rose red serves as a warm complementary accent, reflecting the human and personal side of skincare. Together the two colors form a distinctive and consistent visual identity across all brand touchpoints.

Nizza-Klassifikation

International trademark classification (Nice Classification, 12th Edition) applicable to the Skincare4U brand.

Class Category Relevance to Skincare4U
Klasse 3 Cosmetics & Cleaning Preparations Skincare products, moisturisers, cleansers, serums, and sunscreens recommended or tracked within the app fall under this class.
Klasse 42 Scientific & Technological Services The AI-powered skin analysis software and all digital services provided by the Skincare4U platform are classified here.
Klasse 44 Medical & Beauty Services Dermatologist-backed recommendations, health-related skincare advice, and any consultation features of the app fall within this class.
